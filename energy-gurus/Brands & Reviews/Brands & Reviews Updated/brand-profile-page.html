About Growatt

Growatt designs and manufactures string inverters, hybrid inverters, and residential and commercial energy storage systems, with a product range spanning off-grid, on-grid, and hybrid solar applications. The company operates manufacturing facilities across multiple sites and supports installers and distributors through regional technical and after-sales teams.
