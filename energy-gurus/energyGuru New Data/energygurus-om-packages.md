## What's Included, Per Tier

All tiers include remote performance monitoring and automated fault alerts as the baseline. **All packages default to a 12-hour monitoring cycle.** 24/7 monitoring can be added to any tier at double the price, if the customer chooses it.

**Fault response time: within 24 hours, on all packages.**

**Panel cleaning is not bundled into any tier — it is always a paid add-on**, billed separately regardless of package level.

**Repair/complaint visits are billed separately from the monitoring package** — the monthly fee covers monitoring and scheduled checkups only. Any callout visit for a fault, plus any equipment replaced or installed during that visit, is charged to the customer separately.

### Basic — Annual Checkup
- 12-hour cycle remote monitoring (24/7 available as add-on, double price)
- 1 on-site inspection per year
- Panel, inverter & battery diagnostics each visit
- Automated fault alerts (email)
- Fortnightly performance report
- Fault response: within 24 hours

### Moderate — Bi-Annual Checkup
- Everything in Basic
- 2 on-site inspections per year
- Automated fault alerts (email + SMS)
- Weekly performance report
- Fault response: within 24 hours

### Comprehensive — Quarterly Checkup
- Everything in Moderate
- 4 on-site inspections per year
- Daily performance report
- Fault response: within 24 hours
