EnergyGurus Website Design 
Domain: www.energygurus.online 
Purpose: A professional, credible hub for podcast episodes and resources focused on 
Pakistan’s energy landscape — delivering analysis and recommendations to end users, 
policymakers, and energy solution providers. Includes dedicated sections for Energy Audit 
services, Online Monitoring (real-time telemetry & dashboards), and Operations & 
Maintenance (O&M) services. 
Core goals 
1. Showcase the podcast as the central content driver — easy discovery, listening, 
and subscription. 
2. Present Energy Audit, Online Monitoring, and O&M offerings clearly with service 
pages, case studies, and lead capture. 
3. Build trust with policymakers and solution providers via whitepapers, policy notes, 
and data-driven insights. 
4. Convert visitors into subscribers, leads (for audits/O&M), and podcast listeners. 
Target audiences 
• Residential & commercial end users researching solar, storage, and energy 
efficiency. 
• Policymakers and regulators seeking independent analysis and policy 
recommendations. 
• Energy solution providers, EPCs, manufacturers and investors. 
• Researchers, students and media looking for credible commentary on Pakistan 
energy issues.

Homepage — Wireframe & Content Blocks 
1. Topbar / Utility: language toggle (Urdu/EN), contact phone, quick ‘Request Audit’ 
button. 
2. Header / Nav: logo (EnergyGurus), nav links, search icon, podcast subscribe CTA. 
3. Hero: large headline + subhead 
o Headline: “Energy insights for Pakistan — analysis, action, impact.” 
o Subhead: “Weekly podcast, expert audits, real‑time monitoring and O&M 
services for smarter energy decisions.” 
o Primary CTAs: Listen to latest episode | Request an Audit 
o Visual: podcast host image or illustrative hero (solar + grid + analytics) 
4. Latest Episode preview: audio player (embedded), short show notes, link to 
episode page. 
5. Services quick links: Audit | Online Monitoring | O&M — 3 cards with short bullets + 
CTA. 
6. Why EnergyGurus: 3–4 trust points (independent analysis, policy engagement, 
certified auditors, partner logos). 
7. Featured Resources / Policy Brief: carousel of latest whitepaper or insight. 
8. Case Studies / Results: short outcomes/stats (kWh saved, % uptime 
improvement). 
9. Subscribe & Social: newsletter signup, podcast subscription links, social proof 
(reviews). 
10. Footer: links, contact, small sitemap, legal. 
Podcast Section — Details & Best Practices 
• Episode Listing: card layout with image, title, guest, duration, tags, short excerpt, 
play button and “Read Transcript” link. 
• Filtering: by topic (Policy, Residential, Industry, Storage), by guest type (Regulator, 
Academic, Installer), and by date. 
• Episode Page: 
o Embedded audio player (hosted on site + mirror on podcast host) 
o Download MP3 link 
o Show notes with timestamps (chapter markers) 
o Full transcript (SEO & accessibility) 
o Links to resources mentioned, guest bios, social links 
o CTA: “Request Audit” or “Request a Demo” if episode mentions services 
o Share buttons and subscribe options 
• Schema & SEO: implement Podcast structured data (RSS feed, episode schema) 
and include transcript snippets for better search visibility. 
Audit — Service Page Content 
Sections to include: - Overview: what an energy audit includes (site visit, data logging, solar 
feasibility, load analysis). - Process: Discovery → On‑site data collection → Analysis → 
Recommendations → Report & ROI - Deliverables: sample dashboard, PDF report, 
prioritized measures, payback analysis. - Pricing: packaged pricing + custom quotes for 
bigger systems. - Lead capture: “Request an Audit” form capturing contact details, site 
address, system details, budget range. - Case Studies / Sample Report (downloadable, 
gated for lead capture). - FAQ (timeline, certifications, travel, scope). 
Online Monitoring — Platform Features 
• Real-time & historical telemetry (power, energy, SOC, inverter status) 
• Alerts & notifications (SMS, email, webhook) 
• Custom dashboards for site, portfolio and region 
• API & integrations (inverter brands, IoT gateways, weather) 
• Analytics: performance ratio (PR), downtime tracking, yield vs expected 
• Role-based access (owner, O&M, Distributor) 
• Pricing: per-site, per-asset, or portfolio subscription tiers 
• Demo: embed read-only sample dashboard or screenshots (use image carousel) 
Operations & Maintenance (O&M) 
• Offerings: preventive maintenance, corrective maintenance, spare parts 
management, performance optimization 
• Transparent SLAs: response time, resolution time, uptime guarantees 
• Reporting cadence: weekly/monthly performance reports, quarterly site visit 
reports 
• Contract models: retainer-based, pay-per-visit, outcome-based (performance 
guarantees) 
• Customer portal: tickets, technician dispatching, history logs 
Lead Capture & Conversion 
• Optimized forms on Audit, Online Monitoring and O&M pages 
• Micro-conversions: newsletter signup, episode email signup, download whitepaper 
• Live chat / WhatsApp integration for quick queries 
• Exit-intent modal offering “Free site audit checklist” in exchange for email 
UX / UI Guidelines 
• Clean, modern layout, mobile-first responsive design 
• Typography: Robust readable typeface for Urdu+English support 
• Colors: professional energy-themed palette (sample below) 
• Accessibility: keyboard navigation, transcripts, alt text, color contrast 
Sample palette: - Primary: Deep teal (#006d6d) or rich green-blue - Accent: Solar yellow 
(#FFC84D) - Neutral: Off-white (#F7F9FA), Dark Slate (#2D3A3A) 
Typography: - Headings: Inter or Poppins (supports Latin) - Body: Noto Sans (good Urdu 
support) for bilingual content 
Technical & Hosting Recommendations 
• CMS: WordPress (for ease) or Next.js + headless CMS (Strapi/Sanity) for scalability 
• Podcast hosting: Transistor, Libsyn, or Podbean (host RSS + analytics) 
• Monitoring platform: integrate with InfluxDB/Grafana if building custom, or use 
ready solutions (Emoncms, Solar Analytics, SiteWatch) 
• Analytics: Google Analytics 4 + Search Console, and Hotjar for UX insights 
• Security: HTTPS, CSP headers, regular backups 
• Performance: CDN for audio files and large assets, lazy loading for images
