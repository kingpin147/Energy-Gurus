Final Website Structure 
1. Podcast 
A dedicated section for industry discussions and expert interviews. 
Features 
• Embedded YouTube podcast videos  
• Guest name & designation  
• Episode title  
• Short description  
• Share buttons  
• YouTube channel link  

2. EPCs / Installers 
Directory of certified EPC companies and installers. 
EPC Profile Includes 
• Company profile  
• Logo  
• About section  
• Work portfolio/images  
• Customer ratings & reviews  
• Social media icons  
• Website icon /links
• InMail/contact option 

EPC Dashboard 
Separate login for each EPC/Installer to: 
• View customer inquiries  
• Reply to messages  
• Upload portfolio  
• View ratings & feedback 

3. Brands 
Directory of solar and energy brands. 
Brand Profile Includes 
• Brand profile  
• Representatives  
• Customer care numbers  
• Product showcase (without pricing)  
• Datasheets upload  
• Customer ratings & reviews  
• Reply to comments  
• Social media icons  
• Website icon  
• InMail/contact option 

Product Verification Tab 
Inside every brand profile: 
• Product serial number verification  
• QR verification  
• Warranty verification link  

Brand Dashboard 
Separate login for each brand to: 
• Manage products  
• Upload datasheets  
• View inquiries  
• Reply to customer feedback  
• Manage verification section  


4. EnergyGurus.Online Live QA Session 
Weekly live Q&A session on trending industry topics. 
Features 
• Weekly topic announcement  
• Live YouTube session embed/link  
• Audience questions  
• Guest expert participation  
• Previous sessions archive  
Example Topics 
• Net Metering  
• Hybrid Inverters  
• Battery Storage  
• Fake Products  
• Solar Policies  
• Installation Standards
